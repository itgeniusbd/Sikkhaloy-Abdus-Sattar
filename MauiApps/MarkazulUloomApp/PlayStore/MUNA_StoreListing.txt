MUNA is the mobile app of Markazul Uloom Nurani Academy (মারকাজুল উলুম নূরানী একাডেমী), Chattogram, for its students, guardians and teachers. Powered by the Sikkhaloy education-management platform, it brings the academy's information to your phone in one place.

After logging in with the account provided by the academy, you can see:
• Dashboard – a quick overview of your account
• Detailed information – student profile and class details
• Individual exam & cumulative exam results
• My Report – progress reports
• Accounts – fee status and online fee payment
• Attendance – daily attendance records
• Class routine and exam routine
• SMS inbox – messages sent by the academy
• Notices and homework
• Complaints & suggestions – send feedback to the academy
• Change password
• Photo upload – take a photo with the camera or pick one from the gallery wherever the portal asks for it

Why MUNA?
• Simple, fast and easy to use, in Bangla and English
• Secure login over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection
• No ads
• Your data is never sold

Login accounts are created by the academy. If you don't have a username or password, please contact the academy office.

H. DATA SAFETY FORM
======================================================================
Google requires disclosing data collected through a WebView that loads your own
(controlled) content. The app code collects nothing itself, but the Sikkhaloy
pages it opens do, so declare the following:

Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? -> Yes
Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? -> Yes (HTTPS only; cleartext is disabled)
Which ways can users request that their data is deleted?
   -> "Users can request that data be deleted" by contacting the academy
      (markazululoomnuraniacademy@gmail.com / 01916179333).
   Account creation inside the app: No (accounts are created by the institution),
   so the "delete account URL" requirement for in-app sign-up does not apply. If
   the console still asks for a URL, use the privacy policy URL (section 7 there
   explains how to request deletion).

Data types to tick (all: Collected = Yes, Shared = No, Processed ephemerally = No,
Required (users can't opt out) = Yes):
 1. Personal info > Name
      Purpose: App functionality, Account management
 2. Personal info > User IDs   (username / student ID used to log in)
      Purpose: App functionality, Account management
 3. Personal info > Phone number   (guardian phone in the student record / SMS inbox)
      Purpose: App functionality, Account management
 4. Financial info > Purchase history   (fee payment records)
      Purpose: App functionality
 5. App activity > Other user-generated content   (complaints & suggestions)
      Purpose: App functionality   · Optional (users choose whether to submit) instead of Required
 6. Photos and videos > Photos   (photo taken with the camera or picked from the gallery
    when the user uploads it on a portal page, e.g. student/guardian photo)
      Collected: Yes · Shared: No · Processed ephemerally: No
      Optional (users choose whether to upload a photo) · Purpose: App functionality
 7. Files and docs – tick ONLY if the Sikkhaloy portal accepts document uploads
    (e.g. PDF); then: Collected, not shared, Optional, App functionality.
Note: Name and Phone number are what the Sikkhaloy account/record holds and sends
back and forth; declaring them is the safe choice (over-declaring is not penalised,
under-declaring is).

Do NOT tick: Location, Videos, Audio, Contacts, Calendar,
Health/fitness, Messages (SMS/email from the phone), Web browsing history,
Device or other IDs, App info & performance (no analytics/crash SDK).
"Shared": No – data goes only to the academy and its service provider Sikkhaloy
(service-provider transfers are not "sharing" under Play's definition).
Card / mobile-wallet details are entered on the payment gateway's own pages and
are not stored by the app or Sikkhaloy, so "Payment info" is not ticked.
(If you know the Sikkhaloy portal stores more – e.g. student photos uploaded by
users or email addresses – add those types too.)
